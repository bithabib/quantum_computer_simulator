\section{Sensitivity to the barren cutoff}
Table~S3 moves the classification cutoff from $-1.5$ to $-3$,
which changes the barren fraction from \BarrenCutmOnepFive\% to
\BarrenCutmThreepZero\%. The AUC stays at or above \AucCutmOnepFive{} for
every cutoff, so the ranking the classifier produces is not tied to the
particular threshold. The regression target is unaffected by this choice.

\paragraph{Trainability versus qubit count.} Figure~\ref{fig:qubittrend} shows the mean label as a function of qubit
count, split by cost locality. Global-cost circuits show a steep, monotone
decay of the mean gradient variance with qubit number; local-cost circuits
decay far more slowly. The shaded bands are one standard deviation of the
label across the heterogeneous circuits at each $n$, not the uncertainty of
the mean, which is much smaller. The dataset therefore contains the
qubit-scaling behavior that the predictor must learn, within the finite range
$n\le12$ where it can be measured exactly.
